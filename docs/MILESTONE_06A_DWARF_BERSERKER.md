# Milestone 06A — Dwarf Berserker visual prototype

Implemented locally on 2026-11-11. Awaiting manual visual approval; no Milestone 06B work.

## Open and inspect

1. Open the existing project with Godot 4.7.2 Mono.
2. Open `res://scenes/art/DwarfBerserkerPreview.tscn` and press **F6** (Run Current Scene).
3. The dwarf is on the left; the unchanged, neutral Fallen Guardian V3 is on the right at the same world scale. The initial view is isometric.
4. Click **Front**, **Side**, **Rear**, or **Isometric**. These select inspection cameras; they reposition the two references horizontally for an unobstructed comparison. Both models always use the same orientation and unit scale.
5. **Inspection** restores orthographic size 3.5; **Gameplay / size 22** uses the existing gameplay camera scale. View changes preserve the selected camera size.

The reusable dwarf asset is `res://scenes/characters/DwarfBerserker.tscn`. Its front is local -Z, soles are at local Y=0, scale is one. Geometry is generated by its `[Tool]` C# script on entering the tree, in the editor and at runtime. No baked mesh or external download is required. Reopening the scene reconstructs the same geometry from fixed, deterministic recipes; generated children are not saved as duplicate scene data.

## Design and geometry

- Completely bald scalp, heavy brow, recessed small eyes, broad nose, cheek masses, a subtle facial scar, and thick neck. No helmet.
- Broad, stocky torso and shoulders; thick exposed arms and short, powerful legs. Leather cuirass, straps, rivets, iron reinforcements, compact rusted shoulder plates, bracers, gauntlets, heavy flat-soled boots, knee plates and irregular leather tassets.
- A separate full beard extends over the chest towards the belt. Thick irregular grooved locks, sideburns and moustache give volume; two unequal three-strand braids and six aged metal rings break up its silhouette.
- A separate oversized bearded axe has a thick forged face, closed bevelled cutting edge, rear counterweight, timber shaft, leather wrappings and iron collars. It rests diagonally across the front of the body without obscuring the face in the frontal view.
- Each hand has a palm, four curved fingers and thumb built around its shaft anchor. The axe shaft radius is 0.025; the fingers curve around that radius. Upper and lower arm lengths are approximately 0.30 each; elbows are constructed from those lengths and the actual hand targets, rather than disguising an offset grip with extreme wrist transforms.
- Native `SurfaceTool` / `ArrayMesh`, smooth-normal ellipsoids and curved tubes, extruded panels and flat bevels. No imported models, textures, plugins, dependencies, skinning, animation or gameplay logic.
- The seven shared materials are Skin, Recess, Leather, Iron, Edge, Hair and Wood, assigned by the reusable dwarf scene. A separate dwarf shader adapts the project's existing procedural weathering technique, with independent colors and roughness; it does not share a mutable shader or material resource with the Guardian. Local noise plus per-vertex variation adds grain, tarnish and subdued skin variation without texture downloads.
- **10,336 triangles, 41 material surfaces in 17 mesh instances, 7 shared materials.** Surfaces are a draw-call proxy, not a GPU-profile measurement; shadows can add passes. Modular limb and equipment separation intentionally costs more surfaces than a fully batched static mesh.

Coordinates below are in dwarf model space, before preview positioning/rotation. Generated meshes are offset into their pivot's local space without changing their intended model-space positions.

| Joint/anchor | Left | Right |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shoulder | (-.320, 1.075, .015) | (.320, 1.075, .015) |
| Hip | (-.170, .570, .025) | (.170, .570, .025) |
| Knee | (-.190, .330, -.005) | (.190, .330, -.005) |
| Ankle | (-.190, .130, .015) | (.190, .130, .015) |
| Axe grip | (-.2024, .744, -.430) | (.092, 1.080, -.430) |
| Wrist | (-.2024, .744, -.355) | (.092, 1.080, -.355) |

Elbow positions are calculated deterministically as the midpoint of shoulder/wrist plus an outward/downward bend perpendicular to their axis, with bend length `sqrt(.30² - distance²/4)`. This creates the asymmetric two-handed pose from explicit targets. The axe runs from (-.460, .450, -.430) to (.460, 1.500, -.430). Grip anchors are at 28% and 60% along that shaft.

The beard follows the head pivot. The axe is deliberately a separate component under PoseRoot, not parented to one hand: future two-handed animation must drive the axe and both hand targets together or add an appropriate constraint. There is currently no IK or automatic grip maintenance when joints are moved. Joint preparation is not a claim that an animated rig is already validated.

## Visual review and limitations

Rendered and inspected frontal, side, rear and isometric views, plus true orthographic size 22. Verified a bald crown, broad silhouette, long beard, two-handed diagonal axe, ground alignment and scale comparison. The side view is useful for seeing both hands meet the shaft; the axe blade is naturally edge-on in that view. Initial renders exposed an over-deep circular belt and shoulder flesh protruding through the metal caps; both were corrected before the final captures.

This remains a procedural art prototype: beard locks and braid strands are stylized solid geometry, fingers and facial features are simplified, and some close-up panel transitions show primitive construction. Skin has no subsurface scattering, beard has no strand transparency, and the rigid joints have no deformation. No claim of production-quality anatomy or animated intersection clearance is made. The small facial details, braid rings and armor wear largely disappear at size 22; the broader bald/bearded/axe silhouette remains the intended read. Shadow bias can produce slight visual separation from the floor in close frontal views, despite mesh soles numerically contacting Y=0.

The beard/gauntlets/shaft overlap deliberately in the resting two-handed pose. Large future rotations may intersect the beard, armor, elbows or torso; those require a separate animation phase and visual approval. No animations, collision, navigation, combat, player replacement or playable-demo integration were added. Interactive human review in Godot is still pending; the rendered inspection is not user approval. Do not proceed to Milestone 06B until approval.
